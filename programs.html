AVIATION CAREER LANDSCAPE

Discover roles across the aviation ecosystem.

Explore common career areas. Actual vacancies, eligibility, training and recruitment processes vary by employer and location.

AIRPORT OPERATIONS

Airport Operations Assistant

Support day-to-day airport service coordination, passenger flow and operational administration under employer procedures.

PASSENGER SERVICES

Passenger Service Associate

Assist passengers with check-in guidance, boarding information, service queries and travel-related support.

GROUND SERVICES

Ground Handling Support

Work with ground-service teams on coordination and support activities, subject to required training and authorisation.

CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Airline Customer Support

Respond to customer enquiries, booking-related questions and service requests through approved channels.

RESERVATIONS

Reservation & Ticketing Assistant

Support itinerary enquiries, reservation workflows and ticketing administration where trained and authorised.

ADMINISTRATION

Aviation Office Executive

Assist with documentation, data management, scheduling, reporting and office coordination.

CARGO & LOGISTICS

Air Cargo Operations Support

Support shipment documentation, cargo coordination and logistics administration under applicable procedures.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Aviation Technical Support

Explore technical and maintenance-support pathways that require role-specific qualifications and regulatory compliance.

SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

Safety / Quality Assistant

Assist with records, checklists and quality documentation. Specialist duties require relevant qualifications.

Important: Pilot, air traffic control, aircraft maintenance and other safety-critical or regulated roles require specific licences, certifications, medical fitness and approvals.
